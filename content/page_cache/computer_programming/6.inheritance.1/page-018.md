“Is-a” vs. “Has-a”
• “Is-a” indicates inheritance.
   • Is-a relation: “B is an A.”
   • More specifically, every B has attributes of A.
   • E.g., every CarOwner is a Person.
• “Has-a” indicates composition.
   • Has-a relation: “Every B has an A.”
   • B contains A as a member variable.
   • E.g., every CarOwner has a Car.

Access Modifiers and Inheritance
• An overriding method must provide the same or wider level of
  access compared to the overridden method.
   • Public method → The overriding method must be public.
   • Protected method → It must be protected or public.
   • Default method → It must be default, protected or public.
• Private or static methods cannot be overridden.

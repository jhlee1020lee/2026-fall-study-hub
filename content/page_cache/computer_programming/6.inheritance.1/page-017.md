“Is-a” Rule of Inheritance
• B inherits from A only if every instance of B is an instance of A.
• Consider CarOwner, Person, and Car classes.
• CarOwner inherits from Person, not Car.
   • “Every CarOwner is a Person.”
• Instead, include Car as a member variable .
   • “Every CarOwner has a Car.”

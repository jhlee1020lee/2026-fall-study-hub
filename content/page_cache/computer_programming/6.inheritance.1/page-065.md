Final Class and Method
• final class cannot be inherited.
   • Declare a class final if its definition is complete.
• final method prevents subclasses from overriding or hiding.
   • But they can still be inherited.
• final variable has nothing to do with inheritance.
   • The variable cannot be reassigned after the constructor call.

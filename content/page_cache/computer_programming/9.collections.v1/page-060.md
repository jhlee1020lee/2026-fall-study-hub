Queue Interface
• A list that restricts the ways to add and remove data.
• Queue orders elements in a certain way.
   • First-in-first-out (FIFO) to hold elements before processing them.
      • Think about the numbered tickets in a restaurant.
   • By “importance” (or magnitude) based on the compareTo method.
• Queue supports the insertion or removal of head items.

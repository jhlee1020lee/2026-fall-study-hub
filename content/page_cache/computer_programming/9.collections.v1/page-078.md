TreeMap Class
• TreeMap is sorted by the natural ordering of its keys, or by a
  Comparator provided at the map creation time.
   • Natural order (Comparable, compareTo(Object))
   • Special order (Comparator, compare(Object, Object))
• The implementation also utilizes Red-Black Tree as in TreeSet.

Deque Interface Methods
• void addFirst(E e) / void addLast(E e)
  • Inserts the specified element at the front/end of this deque.
• E peekFirst() / E peekLast()
  • Retrieves, but does not remove, the front/last element.
• E pollFirst() / E pollLast()
  • Retrieves and removes the front/last element.

Encapsulation
• Encapsulation is one of the fundamental concepts in OOP.
  • It bundles “non-visible” attributes with “visible” methods.
• Abstraction
  • Define public interfaces (abstractions) to interact with an object.
  • Hide details under the hood of these abstractions.
• Defensive programming
  • Protect data from an unauthorized access.
  • Hide the internal details of the changes.

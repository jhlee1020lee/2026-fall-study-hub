                  Deep reinforcement learning

                  Reinforcement learning is a branch of machine learning in which an agent learns effective decision-making strategies from reward
                  signals obtained through interaction with an environment. Building on the core concepts of reinforcement learning, this course
 *1. Course       introduces major approaches and recent advances in deep reinforcement learning, which uses deep neural networks, and provides
 Objectives       students with hands-on experience applying them to practical problems through programming assignments. The course will place
                  particular emphasis on applications in robotics. Students will study a range of deep reinforcement learning methods and
                  applications while becoming familiar with the implementation tools and experimental practices used in research and development. By
                  the end of the course, students are expected to have developed the foundational skills needed to understand, implement, and
                  analyze deep reinforcement learning algorithms and apply them to new applications and research problems.

Primary Test
• A primality test is an algorithm for determining whether an input number is prime. Unlike
  integer factorization, primality tests do not generally give prime factors, only stating whether
  the input number is prime or not.


• Factorization is thought to be a computationally difficult problem, whereas primality testing
  is comparatively easy.


• Heuristic Tests: works very well in practice, but does not have a complete mathematical proof
  guaranteeing correctness in all cases.


• Probabilistic Tests: more rigorous than heuristics in that they provide provable bounds on the
  probability of being fooled by a composite number.

Congruence Relation
• Def: Let 𝑚 be a positive integer. For integers 𝑎 and 𝑏, we say 𝑎 is congruent to 𝑏 modulo 𝑚 if
 𝑚 divides 𝑎 − 𝑏. We use the notation 𝑎 ≡ 𝑏 mod 𝑚 .


• Def: The set of all integers congruent to an integer a modulo m is called the
 congruence class of a modulo m.

• Thm: Let 𝑚 be a positive integer. If 𝑎 ≡ 𝑏 mod 𝑚 and 𝑐 ≡ 𝑑 mod 𝑚 , then
                                   𝑎 + 𝑐 ≡ 𝑏 + 𝑑 mod 𝑚 ,
                                      𝑎𝑐 ≡ 𝑏𝑑 mod 𝑚 .


• ℤ𝑚 = 0, 1, … , 𝑚 − 1 is called the residue system modulo 𝑚. We can define arithmetic
 operations on ℤ𝑚 .

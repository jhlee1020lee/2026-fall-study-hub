Number Theory
• Number theory is the part of mathematics devoted to the study of the integers
  and their properties.

• Number theory has long been studied because of the beauty of its ideas, its
  accessibility, and its wealth of open questions.

• Mathematicians have long considered number theory to be pure mathematics,
  but it has important applications to computer science such as cryptography.

Greatest Common Divisor
• Def: Let a and b be integers, not both zero. The largest integer d such that d | a and also
  d | b is called the greatest common divisor of a and b. The greatest common divisor of
  a and b is denoted by gcd(a,b).


• Def: The integers a and b are relatively prime if their greatest common divisor is 1. The
  integers a1, a2, …, an are pairwise relatively prime if gcd(ai, aj) = 1 whenever 1 ≤ i<j ≤n.

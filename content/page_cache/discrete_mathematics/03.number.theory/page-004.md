Euclidean Division
• Let 𝑎 ∈ ℤ and 𝑏 ∈ ℤ>0 . Then there are unique integers 𝑞 and 𝑟, such that 𝑎 = 𝑏𝑞 + 𝑟 and
  0 ≤ 𝑟 < 𝑏.


• Def: In the equality above, 𝑏 is called the divisor, 𝑎 is called the dividend, 𝑞 is called the
  quotient, and 𝑟 is called the remainder. We write
                                           𝑞 = 𝑎 div 𝑏
                                           𝑟 = 𝑎 mod 𝑏

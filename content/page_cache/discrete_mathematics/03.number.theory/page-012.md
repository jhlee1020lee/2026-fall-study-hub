Primes
• Def: A positive integer p greater than 1 is called prime if the only positive factors of p
  are 1 and p. A positive integer that is greater than 1 and is not prime is called
  composite.


• Thm (Euclid): There are infinitely many primes.


• The fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every positive integer greater than 1 can be
  written uniquely as the product of primes where the prime factors are written in order
  of nondecreasing size.

Modular Inverse
• Def: Given two integers 𝑎 and 𝑚 > 0, an inverse of 𝑎 modulo 𝑚 is an integer 𝑏 such that
  𝑎𝑏 ≡ 1 mod 𝑚 , and denoted by 𝑏 = 𝑎−1 mod 𝑚 .


• Thm: An inverse of 𝑎 of a modulo 𝑚 exists if and only if gcd 𝑎, 𝑚 = 1. Furthermore,
  this inverse is unique modulo 𝑚.
  – That is, there is a unique element in ℤ𝑚 that is an inverse of 𝑎 modulo 𝑚, and every other
   inverse of 𝑎 modulo 𝑚 is congruent to it modulo 𝑚.
  – Proof idea: Bézout’s theorem

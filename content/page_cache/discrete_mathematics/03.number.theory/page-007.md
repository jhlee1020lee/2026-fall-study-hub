Base-𝑏 Representations
• Thm: Let b be a positive integer greater than 1. Then if n is a positive integer, it can be
 expressed uniquely in the form:
                              n = akbk + ak-1bk-1 + …. + a1b + a0
where k is a nonnegative integer, a0,a1,…. ak are nonnegative integers less than b, and
ak≠ 0. The aj, j = 0,…,k are called the base-b digits of the representation.


• The representation of n given in Theorem 1 is called the base b expansion of n and is
 denoted by (akak-1….a1a0)b.


• We often omit the subscript when it is obvious from the context

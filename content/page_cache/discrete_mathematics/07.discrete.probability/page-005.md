Applying Laplace’s Definition
• Example: There are many lotteries that award prizes to people who correctly choose a
  set of six numbers out of the first n positive integers, where n is usually between 30 and
  60. What is the probability that a person picks the correct six numbers out of 40?

• Solution: The number of ways to choose six numbers out of 40 is
          C(40,6) = 40!/(34!6!) = 3,838,380.

• Hence, the probability of picking a winning combination is 1/ 3,838,380 ≈ 0.00000026.

 The Probability of Complements and Unions of Events
• Theorem 1: Let E be an event in sample space S. The probability of the event = S − E, the
  complementary event of E, is given by

• Proof: Use the fact that 𝐸 = 𝑆 − 𝐸

  one of these bits is 0?

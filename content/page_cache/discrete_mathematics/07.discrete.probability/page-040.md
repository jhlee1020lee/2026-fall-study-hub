 Independent Random Variables
• Definition: The random variables X and Y on a sample space S are independent if
                          p(X = r1 and Y = r2) = p(X = r1)∙ p(Y = r2).

• Theorem: If X and Y are independent variables on a sample space S, then E(XY) = E(X)E(Y).

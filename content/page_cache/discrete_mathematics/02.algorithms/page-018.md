Algorithmic Paradigms
• An algorithmic paradigm is a general approach based on a particular concept for
  constructing algorithms to solve a variety of problems.
  – Brute-force algorithms : solve a problem in the most straightforward manner, without taking
   advantage of any ideas to make the algorithm more efficient.
  – Greedy algorithms
  – Divide-and-conquer algorithms
  – Probabilistic algorithms
  – Dynamic programming
  – Backtracking
  – And more

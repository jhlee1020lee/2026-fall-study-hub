 Recent Evolutions
• AlphaTensor (2022): Specialized AI via Reinforcement Learning
  – It reframed discovering matrix multiplication algorithms as a single-player "TensorGame" using
   reinforcement learning (RL) and Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) with a neural network guiding
   exploration.
  – For 4 x 4 matrices over the finite field ℤ2 , it reduced scalar multiplications from 49 (Strassen’s
   algorithm) to 47.
  – Also improved 5 x 5 multiplication to 96 steps from 98, later refined externally to 95 by other
   researchers
  – Delivering 10-20% speedups on specific chips, but valid only in binary arithmetic, limiting its
   applicability

Big-Theta Notation
• Def: Let f and g be functions from the set of integers or the set of real numbers to the
  set of real numbers. We say that f(x) = Θ(g(x)) if f(x) = O(g(x)) and f(x) = Ω(g(x)).

  – We say that “f(x) is big-Theta of g(x)” and also that “f(x) is of order g(x)”, “f(x) and g(x) are of
   the same order”.
  – When f(x) = Θ(g(x)) it must also be the case that g(x) = Θ(f(x))

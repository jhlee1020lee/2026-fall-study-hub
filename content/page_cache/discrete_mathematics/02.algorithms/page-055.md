Complexity of Problems
• Class NP Hard: “at least as hard as the hardest problems in NP”.
  – Important: They do not have to belong to NP. They do not need to be decision problem.
  – Example: Traveling Salesman (optimization)
     Problem:
     • Starting from city 1, the salesman must travel to all cities oncebefore returning home
     • The distance between each city is given, and is assumed to be the same in both directions
     • Only the links shown are to be used
     • Objective - Minimize the total distance to be travelled

 The Pigeonhole Principle
• Pigeonhole Principle: If k is a positive integer and k + 1 objects are placed into k boxes,
  then at least one box contains two or more objects.

• Proof: We use a proof by contraposition. Suppose none of the k boxes has more than
  one object. Then the total number of objects would be at most k. This contradicts the
  statement that we have k + 1 objects.

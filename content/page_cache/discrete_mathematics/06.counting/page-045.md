Quiz
How many ways are there to seat four people around a circular table, where two seatings
are considered the same when each person has the same left and right neighbor?
  –If we seat 4 people in a line:
                                       4! = 24
  But this overcounts for a circle.
  –In a circle:
  A–B–C–D
  B–C–D–A
  C–D–A–B
  D–A–B–C
  –So, divide by 4:
                                      4!
                                         = 3! = 6
                                      4

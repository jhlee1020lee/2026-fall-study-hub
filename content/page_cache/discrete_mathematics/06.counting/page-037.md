• Each user on a computer system has a password, which is six to eight characters long,
  where each character is an uppercase letter or a digit. How many possible passwords
  are there?

  – We will determine separately the numbers P6, P7 and P8 of passwords with 6, 7 and 8
   characters respectively.
  – By the sum rule, P = P6 + P7 + P8

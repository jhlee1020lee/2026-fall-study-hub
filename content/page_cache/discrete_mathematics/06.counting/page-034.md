Examples
• How many different license plates can be made in Korea?




  –First two digits: From 00 to 99 → 100 choices
  – Korean character: There are about 32 commonly used Hangul characters for plates
   (e.g., 가, 나, 다, …)
  – Last four digits: From 0000 to 9999 → 10,000 choices
  –Total Number of Plates
                            100×32×10,000 = 32,000,000

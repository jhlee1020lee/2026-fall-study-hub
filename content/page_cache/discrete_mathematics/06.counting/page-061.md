• Show that for every integer n there is a multiple of n that has only 0s and 1s in its
  decimal expansion.
   – Idea: Consider the n + 1 integers 1, 11, 111, …., 11…1 (where the last has n + 1 1’s).

                                      𝑎* = 111 ⋯ 1
                                           * digits
  Now look at:
                                  𝑎$ , 𝑎) , … , 𝑎!#$
  Each has a remainder when divided by 𝑛:
                                      𝑎* mod 𝑛
  There are only 𝑛 possible remainders:
                                  0,1,2, … , 𝑛 − 1

  Distributing Objects into Boxes
• Many counting problems can be solved by counting the ways objects can be placed in boxes.
  – The objects may be either different from each other (distinguishable) or identical
   (indistinguishable).
  – The boxes may be labeled (distinguishable) or unlabeled (indistinguishable).

• Distinguishable boxes.
  – There are n!/(n1!n2! ∙∙∙nk!) ways to distribute n distinguishable objects into k
    distinguishable boxes.
  – There are C(n + r − 1, n − 1) ways to place r indistinguishable objects into n distinguishable
    boxes.

Basic Counting Principles: The Product Rule
• The Product Rule: A procedure can be broken down into a sequence of two tasks. There
  are n1 ways to do the first task and n2 ways to do the second task. Then there are n1∙n2
  ways to do the procedure.

• Example: Two new employees join a company and there are 4 empty offices. How many
  different ways are there to assign offices to them?

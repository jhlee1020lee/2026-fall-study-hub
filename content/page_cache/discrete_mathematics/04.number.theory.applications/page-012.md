   Error Correcting Codes – Erasure Errors
• We will consider two situations in which we wish to transmit information on an unreliable channel.

• Erasure errors
   – The first is exemplified by the internet, where the information (say a file) is broken up into packets,
    and unreliability manifests as packet loss during transmission
   – Suppose that the message consists of 𝑛 packets and at most 𝑘 packets may be lost

• Question: How to encode the initial message into some packets so that the recipient can always
  reconstruct the message from any 𝑛 received packets?

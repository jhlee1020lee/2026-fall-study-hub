Pseudorandom Numbers
• Randomly chosen numbers are often needed for computer simulations
  – It is very difficult to generate a long, uniform string
  – Numbers generated by systematic methods are not truly random, they are called pseudorandom
   numbers.


• The linear congruential method : the most commonly used procedure
  – Choose the modulus 𝑚 and three integers 𝑎 (multiplier), 𝑐 (increment), and 𝑥0 (seed) in ℤ𝑚
  – Generate a sequence of pseudorandom numbers 𝑥𝑛 𝑛≥0 by successively using the recursion:
                                  𝑥𝑛+1 = 𝑎𝑥𝑛 + 𝑐 mod 𝑚


  – Unfortunately, pseudorandom numbers generated by this method do not satisfy some important
   statistical properties of truly random numbers

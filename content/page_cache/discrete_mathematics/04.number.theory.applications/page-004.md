Symmetric-key Cryptography
• Classical encryption schemes are symmetric-key cryptosystems
  – All parties who wish to communicate using a symmetric-key cryptosystems must share the key in
   advance and keep it secretly


  – Example:
    • Alice and Bob agree on a secret key = K
    • Alice encrypts: 𝐶 = 𝐸𝐾 𝑀
    • Bob decrypts: 𝑀 = 𝐷𝐾 𝐶

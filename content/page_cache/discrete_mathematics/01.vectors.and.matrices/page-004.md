Matrix addition
• Definition: Let 𝑨 = 𝑎𝑖𝑗 and 𝑩 = 𝑏𝑖𝑗 be m × n matrices. The sum of 𝑨 and 𝑩,
  denoted by 𝑨 + 𝑩, is the m × n matrix that has 𝑎𝑖𝑗 + 𝑏𝑖𝑗 as its 𝑖, 𝑗 -th element. In
  other words, 𝑨 + 𝑩 = [𝑎𝑖𝑗 + 𝑏𝑖𝑗 ].
  – Matrices of different sizes cannot be added

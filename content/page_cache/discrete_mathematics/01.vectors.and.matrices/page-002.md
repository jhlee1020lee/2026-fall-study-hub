Overview
• Matrices are useful discrete structures that can be used in many ways. For example,
  they are used to:
   – Describe certain types of functions known as linear transformations
   – Express which vertices of a graph are connected by edges (Chapter 10)

• In later chapters, we will see matrices used to build models of communication
  networks and algorithms based on matrix models

• Here we cover the aspect of matrix arithmetic

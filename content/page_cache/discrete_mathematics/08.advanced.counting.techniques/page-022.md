 Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion
• We developed the following formula for the number of elements in the union of two finite
  sets:
                               𝐴∪𝐵 = 𝐴 + 𝐵 − 𝐴∩𝐵

  – We will generalize this formula to finite sets of any size

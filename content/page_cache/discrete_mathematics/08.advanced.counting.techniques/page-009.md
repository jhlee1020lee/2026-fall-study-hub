Linear Homogeneous Recurrence Relations of Degree 2
• Case 1: Let c1 and c2 be real numbers with c2 ≠ 0. Suppose that r2 – c1r – c2 = 0 has two
  distinct roots r1 and r2. Then the sequence {an} is a solution to the recurrence relation
                                               𝑛       𝑛
  an = c1an−1 + c2an−2 if and only if 𝑎𝑛 = 𝛼1 𝑟1 + 𝛼2 𝑟2 for n = 0,1,2,… , where α1 and α2
  are constants.

BOX 1
Features of the Senior Odyssey Program as Activity Engagement
We designed an intervention for older adults to simulate an engaged lifestyle, the Senior Odyssey Program, and are using it to experimentally test the
effects of activity engagement on a wide array of cognitive abilities and predispositions. Features of the intervention include
  Ill-deﬁned ‘‘long term’’ problems for which solutions are developed through team-based collaboration that provides a substantively complex
   environment (Schooler & Mulatu, 2001)
  Fast paced ‘‘spontaneous’’ problems of different types (‘‘brainteasers’’) that exercise component ﬂuid abilities (e.g., reasoning, working memory,
   visual–spatial processing, ﬂuency, speed of processing) to support problem-solving skills
  Opportunities for creativity to stimulate self-initiated use of component skills as needed
  Friendly competition among teams with awards for performance, so that there are consequences for effective solutions
  Choice of activities
  Collaboration with an established educational program for children and young adults, Odyssey of the Mind (www.odysseyofthemind.org), so as to
   build on existing social structures, enhance the potential for scaling up the program, and promote age integration (Riley & Riley, 2000)

   Implicit Association Test (IAT)                                Implicit Association Test (IAT)

One of psychological methods for measuring implicit
or automatic attitudes
How does the IAT measure attitudes?
   The IAT asks you to pair two concepts (e.g., young &
   good; elderly & good)
   More closely associated two concepts = easier to
   respond to them as a single unit
   The more associated, the more rapidly you should be
   able to respond

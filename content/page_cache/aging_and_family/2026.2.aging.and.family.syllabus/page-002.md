Aging and Family (M2808.002800)                                                              Fall, 2026


 a) Attendance (10%): You are expected to attend every class. If you can’t make it, you
    should provide relevant excuses to the instructor before class. If you miss more than 3
    classes without notification, F will be automatically assigned for the final grade. Each of
    missed classes will have 1-point deduction unless it was notified before class and
    excused appropriately.

 b) Pop Quizzes (25%): Throughout the semester, brief quizzes will be administered at the
    beginning of class (the first 10 minutes). They will not be announced. Material on the
    quizzes will come directly from the lecture slides for the prior week and the readings for
    that week. If you did the reading, you should get the questions right. Missed quizzes
    cannot be made-up in any cases.

 c) Unit Write-Ups (15% × 2 = 30%): These 2 assignments are designed to encourage you
    to reflect on in-class activities and topics by responding to given questions. Each writing
    assignment should be no more than 3 pages (without reference pages). Further
    instructions will be provided in class.

           o Unit Write-Up #1 (15%): Due on 10/19 (Mon), 9:00 AM
           o Unit Write-Up #2 (15%): Due on 11/30 (Mon), 9:00 AM

 d) Team Project (40%): During the semester, 4-5 students as a team will do a research
    project. This is not based on literature reviews and data analyses; rather, this project will
    include exploratory activities to capture older adults’ everyday life-world and their lived
    experiences and voices. To complete team projects, students will (a) present a proposal
    to explain their topic and plans in class (10%), (b) present final results from their project
    in class (10%), and (c) submit the final (written) paper for the project (20%).
           o Mid-Presentation (10%): 10/26 (Mon) & 11/01 (Mon), 9:00 AM
           o Final Presentation (10%): 12/14 (Mon), 9:00 AM
           o Final Paper (20%): Due on 12/18 (Fri), 12:00 PM (noon)

                Relative Performance

“X is n times faster than Y” means
n = PerformanceX / PerformanceY
  = ThroughputX / ThroughputY
or = TimeY / TimeX

“X is m% faster than Y” means
1+m/100 = PerformanceX / PerformanceY

To avoid confusion, focus on this definition of “faster”
  ➟ Performance of Y for case 1
   (TimeX=1) / (TimeY= 0.5) = 2 →“Y is 100% faster than X”
  ➟ Performance of Y for case 2
   (TimeX=1) / (TimeY= 0.66) = 1.5 →“Y is 50% faster than X”

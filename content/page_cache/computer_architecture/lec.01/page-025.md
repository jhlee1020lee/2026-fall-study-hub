                              Dennard Scaling
Dennard Scaling is about “performance” scaling
  Moore’s Law tells us transistors will become smaller
  Dennard Scaling tells us, as transistors became smaller
    ➟ They must switch faster, use less power but achieve the same power density.
    ➟ Expected to double CPU frequency every 1.5 years


Breakdown of Dennard Scaling
  As transistors became too small, following issues arise
    ➟ Current leakage
    ➟ Chip overheating

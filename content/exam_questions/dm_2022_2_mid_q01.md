<pre class="exam-question-text">Question 1.

(a) Write the truth table for the proposition ¬(r → ¬q) ∨ (p ∧ ¬r).

(b) Find a proposition with three variables p, q, and r that is true when at most one of the three variables is true, and false otherwise.

(c) Write a proposition equivalent to p ∨ ¬q that uses only p, q, ¬, and the connective ∧.

(d) Prove or disprove: for all real numbers x and y, ⌊x − y⌋ = ⌊x⌋ − ⌊y⌋.</pre>
